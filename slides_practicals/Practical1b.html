Self-study

During this practicum you were likely lost when trying to read the more complex models, because many operations are foreign. The only way to learn is to look up things in the python code you do not understand. Then once you understand you write it out in your own words in the jupyter notebook Markdown sections or inline comments and save it for future reference (in your personal jupyter notebook).

Do the coding exercises on the landing page again, or take them home with you. It will help you to become more literate in reading model code.
